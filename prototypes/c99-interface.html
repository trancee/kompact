Wire invariants

  • Only unsigned exact-width operations touch packet bits.
  • No packed structs, C bitfields, unaligned loads, or host-endian casts.
  • Validation happens once before direct reads.
  • Rejected writes leave every packet bit unchanged.
  • Handles contain pointers only; they do not own memory.
